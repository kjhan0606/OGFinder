**Limitations**: straight trails only; sensitivity ~1-2 pixel-sigma peak (HUDF ~28 mag/arcsec^2) for 50 %, limited by correlated noise/galaxies; wide (>40 px) or short-and-wide (<12x width) trails rejected by design; mesh background removes part of very wide faint trails; bleed/spike rule is a heuristic (`--keep-bleeds`); interpolation unsuitable for photometry; stack needs registered frames; HUDF false-positive test overlaps and is partly tuned; ~8 s per 700^2 image, ~30 s per 4096x2048 chip.
**Not done**: curved/flickering trails; SDSS run-94 check (2 of 6 r frames gave detections, not inspected); per-object contaminating-flux estimate; mask resampling between dithered frames inside the stack step; blind held-out false-positive set.
