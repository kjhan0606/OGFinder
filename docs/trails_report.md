* *Curved / segmented trails* (`--curved`, tile-wise segments linked into chains): SYNTHETIC arcs in real HUDF pixels, median coverage 3 sigma: 0.55 -> 0.89 (4 deg/100 px), 0.00 -> 0.44 (8 deg/100 px); nothing at 1.5 sigma; occasional spurious segments (mean 492 stray px/run at 8 deg, 6 sigma). Piecewise linear only.
* *Flickering trails*: `duty`/`n_gaps`/`flicker` in trails.json; duty 0.5-0.7 found at 3-6 sigma (7/8, coverage 0.95-0.99), duty 0.3 only 3/8 at 6 sigma. No extra gap-bridging beyond the existing best-run statistic.
* *Contaminating flux* (`TRAIL_FLUX`, `TRAIL_FRAC` catalogue columns): model/truth median 1.05 (16-84 %: 0.90-1.33) over 282 contaminated synthetic cases; RMS error 24.6 aperture sigma (bright objects). Median TRAIL_FRAC 0.062.
* *WCS mask resampling in the stack step* (`--register auto|wcs|none`, GUI "Register by WCS"): functional test with dithers of 5-10 px passes; no real dithered set run.
* *SDSS run-94 real-frame check* (160 frames, 5 bands, multi-band consistency through the WCS, PNG cutouts): the frozen straight detector fired in 37/160 frames (56 detections), 53/56 at the 45/135 deg spike angles = saturated-star spikes, so the real false-detection rate there was 23 % of frames, not 0. Fixed with a looser spike rule (core > 30 x amplitude, falloff ratio 2.5): 11 straight (7 % of frames) + 18 curved-chain segments in 8 frames remain; no satellite trail was confirmed. This fix was made after seeing the frames (not blind) and costs recall on HUDF injections (4 sigma 35/40 -> 32/40; 2 sigma 26/40 -> 22/40; 0/88 false trails unchanged).
* *Held-out HUDF*: 8 unseen 700x700 windows (F105W/F125W; no unseen F160W area exists), 0 detections: a small sample.
**Honesty notes**: the SDSS cutouts were not inspected by a human (an image-description tool was used and gave inconsistent details, so classification rests on the automated band-consistency measurement); the curved finder's defaults are first guesses, not tuned; all injection numbers are synthetic trails in real pixels.
