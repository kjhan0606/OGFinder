# Stacking and mean radial profiles (plugin `stacking`, library `ogfkit/stacking.py`)

Measure: *Stack Sources / Mean Profile* (parameters in the plugin dialog / `plugins/stacking/plugin.json`), *Stack and Profile Plot…*, *Profile Table…*, *Show Stack Image*.
Headless: `plugins/stacking/stacking.py IMAGE --work DIR (--catalog TSV … | --positions FILE) …` (the step's `cli`; recorded in the session script and replayed identically).

## Pipeline
1. **Positions**: catalog rows (selection by `--numbers`, `--sel-col/--sel-min/--sel-max`, `--isolate R` = no other catalog object within R px) or a `--positions` file (X_IMAGE/Y_IMAGE or x/y 1-based, RA/DEC in degrees through the image WCS, or two bare columns).
2. **Aligned cutouts** `(2 half+1)²`: cubic-spline resampling on the true sub-pixel position (`--align sub`) or an integer shift (`int`); optional rotation by a position-angle column (`--angle-col THETA_IMAGE`: major axes along +x) and rescaling by a size column (`--scale-col FLUX_RADIUS --scale-unit 5`: one size unit = 5 output pixels; surface brightness is conserved). Positions outside the image are rejected, partly outside pixels are masked.
3. **Masks**: the session/user mask and non-finite pixels; `--mask-catalog f` masks every other catalog object with a circle of max(`--mask-min`, f·A_IMAGE·KRON_RADIUS); `--mask-detect n` masks, in each cutout, the n-sigma (smoothed) peaks of the residual about the azimuthal median profile of the target (so the target and a symmetric halo are not detected, but unlisted neighbours are), grown by 2 px and never inside `--protect-r`. Cutouts with a masked core or more than `1 - min-valid` masked pixels are rejected (the rejection reasons are in the summary: crowded fields reject 40-60 % of faint sources, an isolation bias to keep in mind).
4. **Background** per cutout: `annulus` (sigma-clipped median of the outer ring; removes a halo that reaches it, so use `none` + a sky-subtracted image, or `image` + a background map, for halos), `none`, `image`. **Normalisation** `none | flux | peak | column`, **weights** `none | invvar | column`.
5. **Combination** `mean | median | wmean | clipmean` of the unmasked pixels per position of the cutout.
6. **Errors**: bootstrap over the *sources* (`--n-boot`, default 200) for the stack error map (`stacking_err.fits`), the radial profile and the aperture flux; resampling the sources includes all noise correlations (drizzle, spline interpolation) and the source-to-source scatter.
7. **Null stack** (`--null N`): N random blank-sky positions (not within 5 px of a catalog object or a bad pixel) through exactly the same pipeline (same masks, background, bootstrap): its profile/aperture flux is the residual background / masking bias; `--subtract-null` corrects the profile (`I_CORR`) and aperture flux (errors in quadrature).
8. **Products**: `stacking_stack.fits`, `stacking_err.fits`, optional `stacking_cutouts.fits`, `stacking_profile.tsv` (`R`, [`R_UNIT`], `R_LO`, `R_HI`, `N_PIX`, `I`, `I_ERR`, [`NULL`, `NULL_ERR`, `I_CORR`, `I_CORR_ERR`], `SNR`, [`MU`, `MU_ERR`, `MU_LIM3` with `--mag-zeropoint/--pixel-scale`: mag/arcsec² or the 3σ limit where the bin is not significant]), `stacking_summary.json` (n stacked, aperture sum ± bootstrap, magnitude, S/N, rejection reasons, null result), `stacking_plot.png`; catalog columns `ST_USED`, `ST_BKG`, `ST_NORM`, `ST_APFLUX` (aperture flux of each source in its aligned cutout), `ST_MASKFRAC`.

Recipes: **faint-source stack** (positions of sources below the detection limit: `--bkg annulus --mask-catalog 1 --null 300 --subtract-null`, mean = aperture flux per source, S/N grows like √N); **low-SB halo** (sky-subtracted image, `--bkg none --mask-catalog 1 --mask-detect 3 --null 300 --subtract-null`, log-spaced profile out to the cutout edge, `MU`/`MU_LIM3` columns); **mean profile of galaxies of different size** (`--scale-col`, `--angle-col`, `--norm flux`).

## Validation (`plugins/stacking/validation/stacking_validate.py`, reports `stacking_*_report.json`; tests `plugins/stacking/tests`, 14)
* **Faint sources, synthetic truth** (Gaussians σ=2 px, F=3 per source in noise σ=1: individual aperture S/N 0.2; 30 realisations per case; aperture r=8): mean flux unbiased (|bias| ≤ 1.2 σ of the mean in all 12 cases), empirical scatter 1.69 / 0.91 / 0.51 for N = 100 / 400 / 1600 (∝ N^-½; stack S/N 1.9 / 3.2 / 6.5), bootstrap error / scatter = 1.06 / 0.98 / 0.90 (white noise) and 1.33 / 0.99 / 0.96 (noise correlated by a σ=1 px Gaussian, σ_pix unchanged; N=100 error overestimated by 33 % with 30 realisations), pull std 0.97-1.14 (N ≥ 400), constant and log-normal flux distributions alike; median stacks of the same data give 2.65-3.27 (within the noise of the means; the median of skewed flux distributions is not the mean flux - use the mean for fluxes).
* **Sub-pixel alignment** (300 Gaussians σ=0.8 px, undersampled; ideal = same profile exactly centred): peak/flux in r<5 px: ideal 0.222, spline 0.206 (-7 %), integer 0.192 (-14 %); width σ 0.845 / 0.909 / 0.944; centroid of the stack 0.0002 px (spline) vs 0.02 px.
* **Mask handling** (junk values in random smooth bad regions of 0 / 20 / 50 % of the image, F=6, 600 sources): recovered mean flux 6.14±0.21 / 6.08±0.26 / 6.52±0.47 (truth 6).
* **Low-SB halo** (60 bright cores in a 1800² crowded field with 900 neighbours and a sky gradient, halo I = 0.5σ (r/10 px)^-2 out to 70 px, 12 realisations, exact sky removed): *no neighbour mask*: the stack is dominated by the neighbours (profile 0.5-1 σ/pixel) and only the null-corrected profile recovers the halo (0.49±0.25 vs 0.54 at 10 px, errors ~100x larger); *catalog-neighbour mask*: corrected profile 0.544±0.003 / 0.1107±0.0024 / 0.0217±0.0014 at r = 9.7 / 21.5 / 47 px vs truth 0.541 / 0.110 / 0.0229 (+0.6 %, +0.5 %, -5 %); *per-cutout detection mask*: 0.541±0.003 / 0.1107±0.0020 / 0.0216±0.0012 (0.1 %, +0.5 %, -6 %), i.e. a halo of 0.02σ per pixel (47 px) is recovered at ~18σ. A mesh sky model estimated from the same field removes the overlapping halos (it ate 20-75 % of this halo): the sky must be a model that does not include the halo (noise model plugin + a large mesh, or a constant).
* **Real data, error calibration** (null stacks of N blank-sky positions on the HUDF F160W mosaic, drizzle-correlated noise, 6 px aperture, 1 neighbour-mask unit): set-to-set scatter / mean bootstrap error = 1.25 (N=30, 24 sets), 0.74 (N=100, 20 sets), 0.96 (N=300, 6 sets); M51 (600² field, 2-22 sets) 1.12 / 0.78 / 0.72. The null aperture flux is not exactly zero (+0.0015…+0.0054 ± 0.002 per source on the HUDF depending on the positions, z up to 2.5 for N=300): a small residual background bias of the annulus sky, the reason why the null stack should be subtracted.
* **Real data, faint-source stacks** (HUDF F160W-detected catalog split by magnitude, stacked in F160W, F125W and F105W; mean aperture flux per source in counts, bootstrap-error S/N, null subtracted): bin 22-25 (117 used of 282): 2.56 / 2.85 / 2.54 (S/N 15.1 / 16.0 / 15.8); 25-26.5 (171 of 381): 0.454 / 0.528 / 0.487 (S/N 37 / 33 / 32); 26.5-28 (146 of 309): 0.175 / 0.201 / 0.189 (S/N 48 / 37 / 37). The F125W/F160W flux ratio (1.11, 1.16, 1.15) and F105W/F160W (0.99, 1.07, 1.08) rise by ~8 % from the bright to the fainter bins (no truth available for real galaxies; the three bands agree within 16 % and the F160W S/N follows √N).
* **GUI/headless**: `verify_newplugins.tcl` section `stacking` (M51, 20 checks incl. session replay identical) passes.

## PSF from stacked stars
`--psf-stamp` (GUI *Write stacked-star PSF stamp*) writes `stacking_psf.fits`, the recentred median stack of the selected point sources with azimuthal-median wings (see docs/psfex.md, Extensions); usable as `--psf` input elsewhere. Test: `test_psfext.py` (CLI on PSF stars from a synthetic field: unit sum, non-negative, 25 px).

## Limitations
Resampling correlates neighbouring pixel noise (bootstrap errors account for it; the pixel rms of a cutout is ~15 % lower than in the image); cubic-spline resampling smooths undersampled PSFs (7 % peak loss at σ=0.8 px; use `int` for exactly pixel-aligned work); rescaling does not conserve flux and does not smooth before decimating; no PSF homogenisation between sources; catalog masks use A_IMAGE·KRON_RADIUS circles (not segmentation maps); no redshift/weights from external files other than catalog columns; the median is slow for thousands of sources with bootstrap; rejection of crowded sources biases the stack toward isolated sources; the `annulus` background removes large-scale halos.
